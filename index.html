Process

How it works.

01
Brief & moodboard

You send the brief and the products. Look, light and references are agreed before anything gets generated.

02
Cast & styling

Faces are fixed first, so the same person shows up in every shot. Outfits are built from your real pieces. You sign off on both.

03
Shotlist

Every shot planned like a shoot day: framing, light, lens, pose, and what the product is doing.

04
Stills & film

Images and film get made, then every product is checked against the real thing.

05
Post & delivery

Retouch, upscale and every crop you need, from 4:5 to 9:16 and 16:9.

Timing

First looks in 1 to 2 days. An image campaign in about a week, with film 1 to 2 weeks.

Approval

Nothing gets produced until you've said yes to cast, styling and look.

Revisions

Two rounds of revisions included.
